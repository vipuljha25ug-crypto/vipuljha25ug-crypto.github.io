Understanding Logic behind Numbers & Problems

March 23, 2026

I already knew a little about binary from my first semester, so this unit didn’t feel completely new. But earlier I was mostly just solving questions step by step without really understanding what was going on. This unit also included number systems like decimal and hexadecimal, and topics like modeling, abstraction and optimization. At first it felt like a lot of things together, so I got a bit confused. This time things felt different. In the beginning I was still making mistakes, especially in binary to decimal. Sometimes I missed a power or did a wrong calculation. It was a bit annoying honestly. But after doing more problems, it slowly started making sense. Modeling and abstraction were new for me. At first I didn’t understand why we need to ignore details. It felt confusing. But when the chai example was explained, it became easier. After that I could understand how simplifying things actually helps. Optimization problems were also not easy. In questions like knapsack, I got stuck and didn’t know what to choose. I even got wrong answers a few times. Later I talked with my friends and tried again, then I understood that these problems need more thinking and patience. Overall, I feel I improved in this unit. Now I try to understand what is happening instead of just following steps.
