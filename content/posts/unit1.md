In this unit we learned about data, information, knowledge and wisdom using the DIKW model.I had seen these words before but never took them seriously until more recently. At the start I thought it was relatively easy to get your head around as there seemed to be a basic means of working through the process step by step. I was later confused due to my difficulty in distinguishing between data and information; on some occasions I found that they could appear to be one and the same. The different presentations of the same data in two separate examples shown in class opened my eyes to the idea that we could potentially view results differently than the original presentation indicated. I did believe that having more data would make it possible for you to gain valuable knowledge however after engaging in this unit my belief changed as I now believe that not all additional information will automatically equal a higher level of understanding, it is more based upon how well we comprehend and utilise our newly acquired knowledge. Therefore, obtaining more of data alone does not guarantee you will receive value from that data or that it will result in good decisions. At first I found it difficult to follow what was being told in class, however once I began discussing it all with a few friends I found I could follow up at class easier. Through comparing my opinions with theirs helped me reflect upon what we had studied and allowed me to gain additional thinking as to how other people interpret different material. As a result after having completed this unit I have begun to reflect on social issues differently. I now have more of a desire to process the information through critical thinking than simply accepting things as they were presented to me.
